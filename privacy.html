Privacy Policy — INFLUENTIAL HUNTER Tools

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This is a personal, single-user application. It does not collect, store or share any data about TikTok users other than its owner (INFLUENTIAL HUNTER).

What is accessed, and why

For the owner's own account only, the application accesses what TikTok exposes through the approved scopes (basic profile information and performance data of published posts) and publishes videos on the owner's instruction. These tokens and data are processed by the owner's automation platform, Composio (composio.dev), under their own privacy policy, and are used solely to operate the publishing and analytics features described in our Terms of Service.

No advertising

No data is sold, shared with advertisers or used for advertising purposes.

Revocation

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok settings (Security & permissions), after which all access stops immediately.
